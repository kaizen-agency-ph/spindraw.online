When to stay with Wheel of Names

For classroom spinning, saving many wheels to an account, or sharing wheels with others, Wheel of Names is excellent and more mature.

When SpinDraw fits better

When you’re giving something away and people need to trust the result: a company raffle, a family draw, a community giveaway or a live stream. People often suspect draws are rigged. SpinDraw shows a commitment code before the spin, mixes in a word someone else picks, and lets anyone replay the draw afterwards. It also handles the messy parts of real raffles: pasting raw comments, filtering by keyword, several prizes in a row and winners who never reply.

Questions

Is SpinDraw affiliated with Wheel of Names?

No. SpinDraw is an independent tool. Wheel of Names is a trademark of its owner.

Can I import my list from Wheel of Names?

Yes. Copy the names from your wheel and paste them into SpinDraw, one per line.

Does SpinDraw have accounts?

No. Everything is saved in your browser, so there’s nothing to sign up for.

Baby shower raffle ideas, from the diaper raffle to the draw

By Vincent Carl Benlot · 7 November 2026 · 3 min read

Quick answer

The most popular baby shower raffle is the diaper raffle: guests who bring a pack of diapers get a raffle ticket, and a few names are drawn at the end for small prizes. It helps the parents-to-be stock up and gives guests a reason to stay for the draw.

How does a diaper raffle work?

It’s simple. On the invitation, ask guests to bring a pack of diapers if they’d like to enter. When they arrive, they put their name down and get a raffle ticket. Towards the end of the baby shower, you draw one or more winners for small prizes. The parents-to-be go home with a stack of diapers, and nobody minds, because babies get through a lot of them.

A few details make it run smoothly:

  • Suggest a mix of sizes. Newborn sizes are outgrown fast, so bigger sizes are often more useful.
  • Decide on extra entries in advance. For example, one entry per pack. Write it on the invitation so it’s clear.
  • Keep it optional. Make sure nobody feels they have to buy their way into the fun.

What to write on the invitation

Short and friendly works best. A few lines you could adapt:

  • “Bring a pack of diapers in any size and we’ll enter you in our diaper raffle!”
  • “Want to win a prize? Bring a pack of diapers and get a raffle ticket at the door.”
  • “One pack of diapers, one raffle ticket. Bigger sizes are very welcome!”

Baby shower raffle prize ideas

The prizes are for guests, not the baby, so think small treats:

  • A coffee shop or bakery gift card
  • A scented candle or a small plant
  • A bottle of wine, or a nice sparkling juice
  • A pamper kit with a face mask and hand cream
  • Chocolates or a box of local treats
  • A cinema voucher

Two or three prizes is plenty for most showers. Draw the smaller ones first and save the best for last.

Other baby shower raffle ideas

  • Wipes raffle. The same idea, with packs of wipes instead of diapers.
  • Book raffle. Guests bring a children’s book instead of a card, signed inside, and get a ticket. The baby gets a whole library.
  • Game winners’ draw. Everyone who wins a shower game gets a raffle entry for one bigger prize at the end.

How to draw the winners

Paper tickets in a bowl work fine. If you want a bit more of a moment, or some guests are joining online, put the draw on a screen. In SpinDraw, type each guest’s name, add x2 after the name of anyone who brought two packs so they get two entries, and draw. Turn on the Easter theme and the balls in the ball race become pastel eggs, which suits a shower nicely. Guests joining by video can watch the same draw on a screen share.

If you’re drawing several prizes, keep winners from winning twice by removing them after each prize. That way, more guests go home with something.

Frequently asked questions

What is a diaper raffle at a baby shower?

Guests who bring a pack of diapers get a raffle ticket. At the end of the shower, one or more names are drawn for small prizes, and the parents-to-be keep all the diapers.

How many diapers should each guest bring for a diaper raffle?

One pack is the usual ask. Some hosts give an extra raffle entry for each additional pack, which they mention on the invitation.

What prizes do you give for a baby shower raffle?

Small treats for the guests, like a coffee gift card, a candle, chocolates or a pamper kit. Two or three prizes is enough for most showers.

Can you do a diaper raffle at a virtual baby shower?

Yes. Guests send diapers to the parents directly or order them online, then you draw the winners live on a screen share during the call.

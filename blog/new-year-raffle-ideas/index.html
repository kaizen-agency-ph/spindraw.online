New Year raffle ideas for your year-end party and lucky draw

By Vincent Carl Benlot · 22 November 2026 · 4 min read

Quick answer

A good New Year raffle has free entry for everyone, lots of small prizes through the night and one big prize near midnight, with the draw shown on a big screen or video call. Below are prize ideas, five draw formats and a simple plan for the night.

What makes a good New Year lucky draw?

A year-end lucky draw is often the most talked-about part of the party, especially at company events. The ones that go well tend to share a few things:

  • Everyone is in. One free entry each, including people joining from home.
  • The prizes are spread out. Many small prizes keep people watching, and one big one gives the night a finale.
  • The draws happen in rounds. A few prizes every hour beats forty in a row while dessert goes cold.
  • Everyone can see the draw. On a big screen or a shared screen, so nobody wonders who arranged it.

Resolution-themed prizes go down well at this time of year: running shoes, a cookbook, a language app subscription or a journal.

Five fun ways to run the draw

  1. Hourly draws, grand prize at midnight. Draw a few small prizes every hour, then save the big one for the countdown.
  2. Twelve prizes for twelve months. Name each prize after a month of the coming year and draw them in order.
  3. A New Year horse race. Every name gets a horse, and with the New Year theme on, the field turns gold and silver with party-hat riders and fireworks. You can let the first three across the line win at once. See the horse race picker.
  4. A last-one-standing battle. Fighters in party hats, top hats and tiaras battle it out, and the last few standing win.
  5. The classic wheel. Quick and familiar, and it handles a company of any size. Our guide to the random name picker wheel shows how.

How to keep a year-end lucky draw fair

Year-end draws often come with big prizes, so expect someone to ask whether it was fixed. Lock the list before the first draw so nobody can be added or removed, ask a guest rather than the organiser for a random word to mix in, and share a verify link afterwards so anyone can check every winner. Our guide on picking a random winner and proving it was fair covers the details, and the office raffle guide covers running it for a team.

Check the rules if people pay to enter

A free lucky draw is usually simple. If guests buy tickets, many countries treat it as a lottery with rules attached. In the UK, the Gambling Commission publishes advice on running raffles during the festive season. Check the rules where you are. This isn’t legal advice.

A simple plan for the night

  1. A week before: collect names and agree the prizes, smallest to biggest.
  2. On the day: paste the names into SpinDraw, add the prizes in draw order and turn on the New Year theme.
  3. At the party: lock the list, ask a guest for a random word, then draw a round every hour.
  4. Just before midnight: draw the grand prize, then post the winners and the verify link in the group chat.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good grand prize for a New Year raffle?

Something people can enjoy in the new year: a trip, a new phone, a large gift card or extra days of leave. Pair it with plenty of smaller prizes so more people win something.

How do you run a lucky draw at a year-end party?

Give everyone one free entry, draw small prizes in rounds through the night, and save the grand prize for the countdown. Show the draw on a big screen so everyone can watch.

How can several people win at once?

In SpinDraw’s Ball Race, Horse Race and Pixel Battle, set “How many winners?” and the first ones across the line, or the last ones standing, all win in one round.

Do I need a permit for a New Year raffle?

Usually not if entry is free. If people pay for tickets, many countries treat it as a lottery with rules or permits. Check the rules where you are.

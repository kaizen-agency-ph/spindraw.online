Office Christmas raffle ideas people actually look forward to

By Vincent Carl Benlot · 7 November 2026 · 4 min read

Quick answer

A good office Christmas raffle is free to enter, has lots of small prizes plus one big one, and ends with a draw everyone can watch, in the room or on a video call. Below you’ll find prize ideas for every budget, five ways to run the draw, and how to keep it fair for people working from home.

What makes an office Christmas raffle work?

The raffles people remember aren’t the ones with the biggest prize. They’re the ones where lots of people won something, nobody felt left out, and the draw was a bit of a show. Get a few basics right and the rest takes care of itself:

  • Keep entry free. Give everyone one entry, or one per year of service if you want a nod to long-timers. Free entry also keeps you clear of most raffle rules (more on that below).
  • Spread the prizes. Ten small prizes beat one expensive one. More winners means more cheering.
  • Draw in front of everyone. Put the draw on the big screen, or share your screen on the call, so nobody wonders whether it was rigged.
  • Include remote staff properly. Their names go in the same list, and they watch the same draw.

Office Christmas raffle prize ideas by budget

Mix a few from each column. Prizes the company can give without spending much, like time off, often go down best.

Draw the small prizes first and save the big one for last. It keeps people watching until the end.

Five ways to run the draw

  1. Names in a hat. Classic and quick, but nobody can check it afterwards, and it doesn’t work for people on a call.
  2. A spinning wheel on the big screen. Familiar, fast, and it handles a whole company of names. Here’s how a name picker wheel works.
  3. A reindeer race. Every name gets a horse, and with the Christmas theme on, they become reindeer with antlers and red noses. The first across the line wins. It takes about half a minute and the room will shout at the screen. See the horse race picker.
  4. A last-one-standing battle. Names become little pixel fighters in Santa hats and elf hats. Good for a crowd who like a bit of chaos.
  5. Everyone wins, in order. If you have a prize for every person, draw the whole list. The order decides who picks their prize first.

How do you run a raffle for a hybrid or remote team?

Put everyone in one list, whether they’re in the office or at home. Share your screen on Zoom, Google Meet or Microsoft Teams so everyone sees the same draw. Give each winner a minute to unmute and claim. If someone has stepped away, redraw that prize rather than holding up the party.

People in other time zones will ask whether it was fair. In SpinDraw you can lock the list before the draw and share a verify link afterwards, so anyone can re-run the draw and check the result came from that exact list. Our office raffle guide walks through it step by step.

Do you need a licence for an office raffle?

A free raffle, where nobody pays to enter, is usually simple. Once people buy tickets, even for charity, many countries treat it as a lottery with rules attached. In the UK, for example, the Gambling Commission explains how to run a lottery with people you work with and which raffles you can run without a licence. Rules differ by country, so check yours if money changes hands. This isn’t legal advice.

A simple plan for the day

  1. A week before: collect names from your staff list and agree the prizes.
  2. The day before: paste the names in, add the prizes in draw order, and pick a theme.
  3. At the party: lock the list, ask a colleague (not the organiser) for a random word, and start the draw.
  4. Afterwards: post the winners and the verify link in the team chat.

Frequently asked questions

How many prizes should an office Christmas raffle have?

A good rule of thumb is one prize for every five to ten people, with lots of small prizes and one big one at the end. More winners keeps more people engaged.

How do I stop the same person winning twice?

Remove each winner from the draw once they’ve won. In SpinDraw, keep Remove winners turned on in the Prizes tab and every name can only win once.

Can remote employees take part in the office raffle?

Yes. Put everyone in the same list and share your screen on the video call so remote staff see the same draw at the same time.

Do we need a licence for an office Christmas raffle?

Usually not if entry is free. If people pay for tickets, many countries treat it as a lottery with rules or permits. Check the rules where you are. This isn’t legal advice.

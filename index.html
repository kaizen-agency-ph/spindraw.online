FAQ

Questions

Is SpinDraw free?

Yes. SpinDraw is free with no sign-up. Ads on the website pay for it, and they never appear on the wheel people watch.

Can I use it for an office raffle or a family draw?

Yes. Paste the names, put the wheel on a big screen or share your screen in a video call, and spin. Afterwards, send the verify link so anyone can check the result, even people who weren’t there.

Can I use the wheel in OBS or Streamlabs?

Yes. Copy the overlay URL from the Stream tab and add it as a Browser source. It has a transparent background and comes in 16:9 and vertical 9:16.

Is the wheel really random?

Every pick is computed with HMAC-SHA256 from a secret 256-bit random seed. The seed is committed on screen before the draw and revealed afterwards, so anyone can replay the result.

How can people know the draw wasn’t rigged?

Before spinning, SpinDraw shows a code that locks the entry list and a secret seed. After the draw, the verify page recomputes every winner, and anyone can compare the code with the one they saw.

Is SpinDraw a Wheel of Names alternative?

Yes. It spins like Wheel of Names and adds a fairness proof, pasting names straight from comments or spreadsheets, several prizes with claim timers, and an overlay for live streams.

Where are my entries stored?

Only in your browser. Nothing is uploaded unless you share a verify link or proof file.

Do I need a permit to run a raffle?

It depends on where you are and how people enter. Free giveaways are usually simple, but raffles tied to purchases are often regulated as sales promotions (in the Philippines, by the DTI), and selling raffle slots can count as gambling. Check your local rules. SpinDraw picks the winner fairly; it doesn’t make a promotion legal.

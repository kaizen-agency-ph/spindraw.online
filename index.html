FAQ

Questions

Is SpinDraw free?

Yes. SpinDraw is free with no sign-up. Tips help keep it running, and if the website ever shows ads, they’ll never appear on the wheel or the games people watch.

Can I use it for an office raffle or a family draw?

Yes. Paste the names, put the wheel on a big screen or share your screen in a video call, and spin. Afterwards, send the verify link so anyone can check the result, even people who weren’t there.

Who decides the winner in the games?

The same locked draw as the wheel, in every game: the Ball Race, Horse Race, Pixel Battle, Slot Machine and Bracket. The races, battle and bracket are real simulations with nothing steered, and the drawn names go on whoever comes out on top. Each person’s chance is exactly their share of the entries, and the verify link works the same way.

Can I play bingo online with SpinDraw?

Yes. SpinDraw online bingo is a free bingo caller for 75-ball and 90-ball games. Players get a card on their phone from a link or QR code, or you print cards, and you check winners by card number. It works in a room or on Zoom, Teams and Meet, with no money involved.

Is there a random name picker game?

Yes. SpinDraw has five: a Ball Race (a marble race with names), a Horse Race, a Pixel Battle, a Slot Machine and an Elimination Bracket. Everyone can watch who’s winning as it happens, and they’re free, with no sign-up.

Do the games give everyone the same chance?

Yes. In many marble race and battle pickers the physics or a fighter’s abilities decide, so the odds aren’t equal. In SpinDraw, a fair draw from the locked list picks the winner first and the game reveals it, so every entry has exactly its share of the chance in every game.

Can more than one person win at once?

Yes, in every game. Set “How many winners?”, up to 50 (or 4 in the Bracket), and the first ones across the line, the last ones standing or the next pulls of the slot machine win. Each winner is a separate fair draw that the verify link checks.

Can I use the wheel in OBS or Streamlabs?

Yes. Copy the overlay URL from the Stream tab and add it as a Browser source. It has a transparent background and comes in 16:9 and vertical 9:16.

Is the wheel really random?

Every pick is computed with HMAC-SHA256 from a secret 256-bit random seed. The seed is committed on screen before the draw and revealed afterwards, so anyone can replay the result.

How can people know the draw wasn’t rigged?

Before spinning, SpinDraw shows a code that locks the entry list and a secret seed. After the draw, the verify page recomputes every winner, and anyone can compare the code with the one they saw.

How long do verify links last?

Seven days after the raffle finishes. After that, the link leads to a page explaining that the details were removed. Hosts can download a proof file, which doesn’t expire, to keep a record they can check at any time.

Is SpinDraw a Wheel of Names alternative?

Yes. It spins like Wheel of Names and adds a fairness proof, pasting names straight from comments or spreadsheets, several prizes with claim timers, and an overlay for live streams.

Where are my entries stored?

Only in your browser. Nothing is uploaded unless you share a verify link or proof file.

Do I need a permit to run a raffle?

It depends on where you are and how people enter. Free giveaways are usually simple, but raffles tied to purchases are often regulated as sales promotions (in the Philippines, by the DTI), and selling raffle slots can count as gambling. Check your local rules. SpinDraw picks the winner fairly; it doesn’t make a promotion legal.

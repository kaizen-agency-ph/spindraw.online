A transparent overlay for OBS

Add one Browser source and the wheel, prize, countdown and winner sit right over your camera. 16:9 or vertical 9:16 for TikTok. A green-screen version works in apps without browser sources.

Fairness anyone can check

Entries get locked with a hash shown on screen, chat picks a salt word, and after the raffle a verify link re-runs every draw in the viewer’s own browser.

Paste comments as they are

Copy a Facebook, TikTok or YouTube comment section and paste it. Likes, timestamps and “Reply” are stripped, duplicates removed, and you can count only comments that say #JOIN.

Several prizes and a claim timer

Queue prizes 3rd → 1st. The winner gets 60 seconds to claim in chat. If they don’t, one key logs the no-show and redraws.

Proof to post

Download a winner card PNG, the results CSV and the full entry list, so there’s no “scripted!” in the comments.

Facebook Live raffle

  1. Ask viewers to comment a keyword.
  2. Copy the comments, paste them into SpinDraw and set the keyword.
  3. Lock, spin, post the verify link as a comment.

TikTok LIVE giveaway

  1. Pick the 9:16 overlay and add it in TikTok LIVE Studio or OBS.
  2. Collect names from comments or a Google Form.
  3. Spin on stream. The winner card fills the vertical screen.

YouTube / Twitch picker

  1. Paste live chat lines (“Name: message”).
  2. Use “1 entry per person” to stop spammers stacking entries.
  3. Give members extra entries with “Name x3”.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Ads on this site pay for it. They never show on the overlay your viewers see.

Where are my entries stored?

Only in your browser. Nothing is uploaded. A verify link only exists if you choose to share one.

How can viewers know the host didn’t cheat?

The commitment hash shown before spinning locks both the list and a secret seed. Chat adds a salt word after that, so the host can’t pre-pick a result. The verify link reveals the seed and recomputes every winner.

Can I use it without OBS?

Yes. Share your screen with the control panel, or open the overlay window with the dark background and share that.

SpinDraw / About

Hi, I’m Vince. Sorry about the horses.

I’m Vincent Carl Benlot, and I made SpinDraw. It started as a simple idea: build a nicer wheel of names for online raffles. Then things escalated, and now there are up to 100 pixel horses on the track.

So why does this exist?

Picking a winner sounds easy until there’s a prize on the line. Someone always asks, “Wait, was that rigged?” Usually as a joke. Usually.

So SpinDraw locks the list before the draw and gives you a link that lets anyone replay the result. Fewer raised eyebrows, more cheering.

Then I figured that if everyone’s already watching, the draw might as well be fun. That’s how a tidy little wheel gained a ball race, a horse race and a pixel battle. No pixel fighters were permanently harmed. They’re all back for your next raffle.

A bit about me

I’m a marketer and web builder from Malaybalay City, in Bukidnon, Philippines. I call myself the technician who became a marketer: I studied IT, then spent the last seven years or so working on marketing and the web with clients around the world.

You can see the full story on my personal site, which has a little game where you walk my pixel self through my career. Yes, I have a pixel problem.

I also own Kaizen

Kaizen is my web and automation studio. We build websites and automations for founders and service businesses, then keep making them better. The name comes from the Japanese practice of continuous improvement, and our motto is “Built sharp. Improved forever.”

If you like how SpinDraw works and want something built for your own business, that’s the place to say hello.

A few house rules

Your names stay with you

No account and no upload. Your list lives in your browser unless you choose to share a verify link or proof file.

Cheating is the hard part

That goes for you, me and your cousin who always wins. The draw is locked first, so anyone can check it afterwards.

Free, and meant to be fun

No sign-up and no paid tier. If it made your raffle better, you can buy me a coffee, but you never have to.

Come say hi

Found a bug or have an idea for the next game? I’d love to hear it.

Random name picker alternatives, compared

The right picker depends on the job. Use Wheel of Names for everyday spinning, Picker Wheel for many kinds of wheel, Comment Picker to draw straight from social posts, Roulette Rush or Namebrawl for huge race and battle games, and SpinDraw when every entry needs the same odds and people need to check the draw was fair.

The best pick for each job

  • Everyday spinning, saved and shared wheels: Wheel of Names. It’s mature, with accounts, cloud saving and an API.
  • Lots of different wheels: Picker Wheel, with yes/no, team, number, letter and country wheels.
  • Drawing directly from a social post: Comment Picker, with dedicated tools for Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, TikTok, X and more.
  • A marble race with a very long list: Roulette Rush, with up to 500 marbles and six maps.
  • A pixel battle with a huge list: Namebrawl, with up to 1,000 fighters, abilities and a team mode.
  • A race you can post as a video: WaveSpeed Random Name Race, which exports an MP4.
  • A game where every entry has the same chance, and people can check it: SpinDraw. The winner comes from a draw locked before anything moves, so the wheel, slot machine, races, pixel battle and bracket all give each entry exactly its share of the odds, and a link replays every pick afterwards.

“Random” and “provable” aren’t the same thing

Almost every picker on this page is random enough. The harder question comes when you’re giving away a prize: how does anyone who wasn’t holding the mouse know the host didn’t re-spin until a friend won, or quietly edit the list?

A strong random generator can’t answer that, and neither can a certificate or a screen recording, because both only show the attempt that got published. What helps is a commitment. The tool locks the list and a secret seed behind a code before the draw, the code is shown to everyone, and afterwards the seed is revealed so anyone can recompute the winner. That’s how SpinDraw works, and it’s why we built it.

Are race and battle pickers fair?

Games are more fun to watch than a wheel, but check how they pick the winner. Many let the physics or the fight decide, so the outcome depends on the course, the start positions or a fighter’s abilities. Roulette Rush’s own site says equal entries don’t guarantee equal winning chances and its results aren’t audited, and Namebrawl says its battles aren’t an equal-probability draw. That’s fine for fun, but not when there’s a prize on the line.

SpinDraw works the other way round. A fair draw picks the winner first, from the locked list, and the race or battle reveals it. So every entry has exactly its share of the chance in every game, and the verify link can prove it. We explain the difference in our guide to fair name picker games.

The commitment has one limit: the host still creates the seed. To make the result hard to steer, ask someone else to pick a word after the list is locked, and mix it in as the public salt.

Where SpinDraw isn’t the best choice

  • No accounts or cloud saving. Lists stay in your browser, so they don’t follow you to another device.
  • The games stop at 100 names. Roulette Rush goes to 500 marbles and Namebrawl to 1,000 fighters. Our wheel handles longer lists, but it isn’t a race.
  • No team battles or fighter abilities. Namebrawl has both. In SpinDraw every fighter has the same chance, on purpose.
  • No direct import from a post. You copy and paste comments, and SpinDraw cleans them up. Comment Picker’s tools are built around drawing from the post itself.
  • No video export. WaveSpeed exports an MP4; with SpinDraw you’d record your screen.
  • It’s new. SpinDraw launched in October 2026, so it has a much shorter track record than Wheel of Names or Picker Wheel.

Questions

What is the best alternative to Wheel of Names?

It depends on the job. Picker Wheel suits people who want many kinds of wheel, Comment Picker suits giveaways drawn from social posts, Roulette Rush and WaveSpeed suit marble races, and SpinDraw suits raffles where people need to check the result.

Which random name picker can prove the draw was fair?

Of the tools here, SpinDraw is the only one whose pages describe a commitment that anyone can check: a code shown before the draw and a verify link that replays every pick afterwards. Comment Picker offers a public results page and a certificate, and Wheel of Names offers a statistical test of its spins.

Is there a free marble race name picker?

Yes. Roulette Rush takes up to 500 marbles, WaveSpeed Random Name Race takes up to 100 names and exports video, and SpinDraw’s Ball Race takes up to 100 names with a verify link.

Which name picker works best for live streams?

Wheel of Names has a dedicated streaming page, and SpinDraw has a transparent overlay for OBS in 16:9 and vertical 9:16. Roulette Rush also describes use on Twitch and YouTube.

Are marble race and battle name pickers fair?

It depends on the tool. Some let the race or fight decide, so the odds aren’t equal: Roulette Rush and Namebrawl both say so on their own sites. SpinDraw draws the winner fairly first and the game reveals it, so every entry has exactly its share of the chance.

A ball race name picker that everyone wants to watch

A marble race for your list of names: every name gets a ball. They drop through pegs, spinning bars and bumpers while a live board shows who’s winning, and the first across the finish line is your winner.

How to run a ball race

  1. Open the picker and paste up to 100 names, one per line.
  2. Choose Ball Race above the wheel and set How many winners? if you want more than one. Pick a length in the Style tab: Quick (about 20 seconds), Normal (about 35) or Epic (about 60).
  3. Click Lock entries in the Draw tab so everyone can see the code before anything moves.
  4. Press Start race (or Space). Share your screen, or click Present for a clean view on a projector or stream.
  5. When enough balls have crossed the line, the winner card appears. Press C if a winner claims their prize, or R to log a no-show. Once everyone in that run is dealt with, a new race replaces any no-shows.

What people see

  • A camera that follows the leaders down a different course every time
  • A live Who’s winning board with the top five
  • A name above every ball, so everyone can follow their own
  • Your own slice colours from the Style tab on the balls

Who decides the winner?

The same locked draw that powers the wheel decides, and the race reveals it. SpinDraw runs real physics from a seed, with nothing steering any ball, finds the ball that comes first, and puts the drawn name on it. Each person’s chance of winning is exactly their share of the entries, and the verify link still lets anyone re-run the draw afterwards.

Good for

Office parties and team draws, classroom picks, family game nights, community giveaways and live streams: anywhere a random name picker game makes the moment more fun than a plain list.

Questions

How many names can race?

Up to 100. For bigger lists, use the wheel, which handles thousands.

Is the race rigged?

No one can steer it. The winner comes from the locked draw, the same as the wheel, and the verify link shows the result came from the locked list. The race is how the result is revealed, and the screen says so.

Do extra entries still count?

Yes. Everyone gets one ball, but “Name x3” still gives that person three times the chance, because the draw uses the entries.

Can I show it in OBS or on a projector?

Yes. Add the overlay as a Browser source, or click Present for a clean view.

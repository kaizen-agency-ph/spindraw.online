A battle royale name picker with pixel fighters

Up to 100 names become little pixel fighters in one arena. They dodge, block and counter each other while the zone closes in, and the last ones standing are your winners: one, or as many as you choose.

How to run a battle

  1. Open the picker and paste up to 100 names, one per line.
  2. Choose Pixel Battle above the wheel and set How many winners? if you want more than one. Pick a length in the Style tab: Quick, Normal or Epic.
  3. Click Lock entries in the Draw tab so everyone can see the code before the fight starts.
  4. Press Start battle (or Space). Share your screen, or click Present for a clean view on a projector or stream.
  5. When only your chosen number of fighters is left, the winner card appears. Press C if a winner claims their prize, or R to log a no-show. Once everyone in that run is dealt with, a new battle replaces any no-shows.

What people see

  • Every person gets their own look, with a body, hat and weapon based on their name
  • Fighters that think for themselves: they circle, dodge, block, counter and back off when they’re hurt
  • HP bars, damage numbers and a live kill feed
  • A counter of who’s still alive, plus a Most KOs board
  • A shrinking zone that keeps the action moving, and a zoom-in on the final two

Who decides the winner?

The same locked draw that powers the wheel decides, and the battle reveals it. SpinDraw runs a real simulated fight from a seed, with nobody steered, finds the last fighter standing, and gives that fighter the drawn name. Each person’s chance of winning is exactly their share of the entries, and the verify link still lets anyone re-run the draw afterwards.

Good for

Team events, classrooms, game nights, communities and live streams. It’s a lighthearted cartoon fight between tiny pixel characters, with no blood or gore.

Questions

How many fighters can there be?

Up to 100. For bigger lists, use the wheel, which handles thousands.

Do stronger fighters have better odds?

No. How a fighter looks comes from their name, and the winner comes from the locked draw. Looks and stats make no difference to anyone’s chances.

Do extra entries still count?

Yes. Everyone gets one fighter, but “Name x3” still gives that person three times the chance, because the draw uses the entries.

Can I show it in OBS or on a projector?

Yes. Add the overlay as a Browser source, or click Present for a clean view.

        Freezes the list and puts a commitment hash on screen. Viewers can screenshot it.

        After locking, type the first word a viewer posts in chat. Taking the first one, rather than picking a word you like, is what makes the result hard to steer.

      3. Spin

        Space to spin. C if the winner claims, R if they don't show up in time.

        Reveals the secret seed. Share the link (or the proof file) so anyone can re-run every draw.

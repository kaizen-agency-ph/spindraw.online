      How to pick a random name from a list

      1. Paste your names in the Entries tab above, one per line. Duplicates are removed unless you turn that off.
      2. To give someone better odds, write Name x2 or Name x3.
      3. Optional: click Lock entries in the Draw tab so everyone sees a code before the spin.
      4. Press Spin or the Space bar. The pointer lands on the winner.
      5. Keep spinning to draw more names. With Remove winners on, nobody is picked twice.

      Picking multiple winners

      The wheel picks one name per spin, which keeps the suspense for every prize. List your prizes in the Prizes tab in the order you’ll draw them (for example 3rd, 2nd, then 1st), and each spin is matched to the next prize. Winners drop off the wheel, a claim timer gives them time to respond, and R redraws a no-show. If you’d rather reveal several winners in one go, the slot machine and the races pick up to 50 in a single run.

      Who uses a name picker wheel

      • Teachers: pick who answers next, or spin until the wheel is empty to set a presentation order. See the classroom guide.
      • Giveaways on Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube: paste the copied comments and count only those with a keyword like #join.
      • Office raffles and lucky draws: put the wheel on a big screen with your logo in the middle. See the office raffle guide.
      • Teams and families: who goes first, who hosts next, who pays for dinner, or a Secret Santa order.

      Is the wheel really random?

      Each pick comes from a cryptographic HMAC-SHA256 of a 256-bit random seed, never from Math.random(). Lock the list first and the wheel shows a code that commits to the list and the secret seed before anything spins. Afterwards, the verify link lets anyone re-run every pick and check it came from that locked list.

      Tips

      • Use Present for a clean full-screen wheel on a projector or a shared screen in Zoom, Meet or Teams.
      • The Results CSV button saves every winner, prize and time for your records.

      Questions

      Is the random name picker wheel free?

      Yes. It’s free and needs no account. If the site ever shows ads, they’ll never appear on the wheel or the stream overlay.

      How many names can the wheel hold?

      Thousands. With very long lists the slices get thin, but every name still has its fair share of the chance.

      Can the wheel pick multiple winners?

      Yes, one per spin. Keep Remove winners on so nobody wins twice, and list your prizes so each spin is matched to the next prize. To reveal up to 50 winners in one run, use the slot machine or a race.

      Can I give someone more entries?

      Yes. Write “Name x3” to give that person three entries, so three times the chance of a single entry.

      Can I pick a name from Facebook or YouTube comments?

      Yes. Copy the comments, choose “Copied comments” as the paste format, and optionally count only comments containing a keyword.

      Are my names uploaded anywhere?

      No. Names are stored only in your browser. They’re shared only if you choose to share a verify link or proof file.

        Quick answer

        SpinDraw online bingo is a free bingo caller with cards. The host shows the caller on a big screen or a video call, players open their own card on their phone from a link or QR code (or use printed cards), and the host checks a winning card by its number. It supports 75-ball and 90-ball bingo, and the call order comes from a locked fair draw anyone can verify afterwards.

        How to host online bingo

        1. Choose 75-ball or 90-ball, and pick what you’re playing for.
        2. Share the player link in your chat, let people scan the QR code on screen, or click Print cards.
        3. Click Lock game so everyone sees the code that commits to the call order. Optionally, ask someone for a public word.
        4. Press Call next or Space, or turn on auto-call. Click Present to show the caller full screen.
        5. When someone shouts bingo, type their card number under Check a card and confirm the win.
        6. When you’re done, click Finish & create verify link and share it.

        Playing on Zoom, Teams, Meet or a stream

        Share your screen and click Present for a clean, full-screen caller with the board, the last calls and the QR code. Paste the player link in the meeting chat. Each person’s phone opens their own card, and they tap numbers as you call them. Their phones don’t need to connect to yours, so it works on any network. For a live stream, show the Present view and put the player link in your description or chat.

        Playing in a room

        Put the caller on a TV or projector. Guests scan the QR code to get a card on their phone, or you hand out printed cards. Calls can be read aloud in the browser’s voice, so a classroom or party can follow along without anyone reading the numbers.

        Printable bingo cards

        Click Print cards to print numbered cards for the current game: two 75-ball cards or six 90-ball tickets per page. Printed cards and phone cards come from the same game code, so you can mix them in one game. Check any of them the same way, by card number.

        Custom bingo patterns

        In 75-ball, choose Custom pattern… under Playing for and click squares on the 5 × 5 grid to draw any shape: a letter, a frame, a diamond or a postage stamp in the corner. Start from a ready-made shape like Letter T, Plus or Frame, then change it, and give it a name if you like. The pattern shows on the big screen so players know what they’re going for, and checking a card works the same way. The free centre always counts as marked.

        One card per person

        For a prize game where fairness matters, turn on One card per person and paste your player list. Each name gets its own numbered card and a personal link to send them, or print named cards. Nobody shares a card or gets a second one, and only assigned cards can win: if someone shouts bingo on a card that isn’t on the list, the check says so. The player list is locked in with the game before the first call, so the verify link also proves nobody was moved onto a winning card.

        Checking a winner

        Type the card number a player shouts out. SpinDraw rebuilds that card from the game code, marks every number called so far and says whether it has the pattern, for example “BINGO! #0042: row 2”, or how many numbers it still needs. Confirm the win and it’s logged with the prize and the call it won on. In 90-ball, the prize moves on from one line to two lines to full house automatically.

        Is the bingo caller fair?

        Yes. When you lock the game, SpinDraw picks a secret seed and shows a code that commits to the call order before the first number. Cards come from a separate public game code, so knowing a card tells you nothing about the calls. When the game ends, the seed is revealed and the verify link lets anyone rebuild every call in order and check each winning card against the numbers called before it won.

        Questions

        Is SpinDraw online bingo free?

        Yes. The caller, phone cards and printable cards are free, with no sign-up and no money involved.

        How do players get a bingo card?

        Share the player link or show the QR code. Each phone that opens it gets its own card, which stays the same if they refresh. You can also print numbered cards.

        Do players’ cards mark themselves?

        No. Players tap numbers as they’re called, like daubing a paper card. Their phones don’t need to connect to the host, so it works on any network.

        Can two players get the same card?

        With the open player link, each phone picks one of 9,999 cards at random, so repeats are rare in small groups but possible in big ones. Turn on One card per person to give everyone their own numbered card instead, so nobody shares a card. Printed cards are numbered in order, so they never repeat.

        Can I make sure everyone only plays one card?

        Yes. Turn on One card per person and paste your player list. Each name gets its own card and personal link, only those cards can win, and the list is locked in with the game, so the verify link shows nobody was moved onto a winning card.

        How do I check if someone has bingo?

        Type their card number under Check a card. SpinDraw shows the card with the called numbers marked and says whether it has the pattern you’re playing for.

        What’s the difference between 75-ball and 90-ball bingo?

        75-ball uses a 5 × 5 card under B-I-N-G-O with a free centre and patterns like lines or blackout. 90-ball uses a 3 × 9 ticket with 15 numbers and prizes for one line, two lines and a full house.

        Can I make my own bingo pattern?

        Yes, in 75-ball. Choose Custom pattern under Playing for, click squares to draw the shape or start from one like Letter T, Plus or Frame, and name it if you like. It shows on the big screen, and card checks and the verify link use it.

        Can the caller read numbers aloud?

        Yes. Turn on Read calls aloud and your browser’s voice announces each number, like “B, 12”.

        Can I use it on Zoom or Teams?

        Yes. Share your screen with the Present view, and paste the player link in the meeting chat.

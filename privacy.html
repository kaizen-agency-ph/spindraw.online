Privacy policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

What stays on your device

Entry lists, prizes, settings and raffle seeds are saved only in your browser’s local storage. They are never sent to our servers. A verify link holds the raffle data in the part of the URL after #, which browsers don’t send to servers. It is only shared if you share it.

Overlay connection

To reach an OBS browser source, the control panel and overlay connect peer-to-peer over WebRTC using the public PeerJS signalling service (peerjs.com). Raffle state then travels directly between the two windows.

Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. See how Google uses data from partner sites.

Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through a Google-certified consent banner before personalised ads load.

Fonts and libraries

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, and the PeerJS library from unpkg.com. These providers may log your IP address when files are downloaded.

When to stay with Picker Wheel

Picker Wheel covers far more than names. Stay with it if you need team or number wheels, several wheels on one page, or files saved to an account so they follow you between devices.

When SpinDraw fits better

SpinDraw does one job: picking winners people can trust. It suits a company raffle, a community giveaway or a live stream, where someone may ask whether the draw was rigged. It shows a code before the draw and gives you a link that lets anyone replay the result.

It’s also more fun to watch. Up to 100 names can race as balls or horses or fight as pixel fighters, with a live board showing who’s winning, while the same locked draw decides the result.

Questions

Is SpinDraw free like Picker Wheel?

Yes. SpinDraw is free with no sign-up and has no paid tier. Picker Wheel is also free, and offers a paid Premium plan with an ad-free experience and more saved files.

Can I move my list from Picker Wheel to SpinDraw?

Yes. Copy your entries and paste them into SpinDraw, one per line. Weights use the format “Name x3”.

Does SpinDraw have a yes or no wheel?

Not a dedicated one. You can type Yes and No as two entries and spin, but Picker Wheel has a purpose-built yes/no wheel.

Is SpinDraw affiliated with Picker Wheel?

No. SpinDraw is an independent tool. Picker Wheel is a trademark of its owner.

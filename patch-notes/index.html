Pixel Battle

How it works →
v1.65 Nov 2026

Smarter around the circle

  • ImprovedFighters now look before they roll, so they no longer dodge out of the circle to their deaths. If there’s nowhere safe to roll, they block instead.
  • ImprovedThey also stop stepping outward near the edge, and stop chasing anyone already outside.
v1.55 Nov 2026

Seasonal themes

  • NewHalloween, Christmas, New Year, Valentine’s, Easter and Summer themes you can switch on or off.
  • ImprovedOne Sound button now turns all game audio on or off.
  • NewFighters dress for the season: witches and skeletons, Santas and elves, party hats, bunny ears and more, in a matching arena.
v1.45 Nov 2026

Countdown and champion’s finish

  • NewA 3, 2, 1 countdown before the start: “Let’s battle!”
  • NewA big “ta-dah!” as the winner card appears.
  • ImprovedThe Most KOs board is now one slim line that fades when fighters walk under it, so it no longer hides the fight.
  • NewA new winner’s scene with a crown, sunburst, pixel sparks and a heroic jingle.
v1.35 Nov 2026

Battle music

  • NewOriginal chiptune music made for the arena, which speeds up for the finish. The Sound button turns it off.
v1.25 Nov 2026

Fighters learned to fight

  • NewFighters now think for themselves: they pick targets, circle, press forward and back off when hurt or outnumbered.
  • NewAttacks have a visible wind-up, so opponents can roll out of the way or raise a guard, then counter.
  • NewSlash arcs, dodge rolls, knockback, and floating DODGE, BLOCK and MISS text.
  • BalanceHealth retuned so most fighters are knocked out in combat instead of being finished by the zone.
  • ImprovedSmoother movement between frames.
  • NewThe verify page now has a match report: length, knockouts, dodges, blocks, most KOs and every elimination in order.
  • ImprovedVerify links now stay open for 7 days. After that, they lead to a page explaining the details were removed, while proof files keep working.
v1.14 Nov 2026

Names and more winners

  • ImprovedEvery fighter shows their name, with smaller labels in crowded fights.
  • New“How many winners?” lets the last few standing all win, up to 50.
v1.04 Nov 2026

Into the arena

  • NewUp to 100 pixel fighters in a shrinking zone, with HP bars, a kill feed and a zoom on the final fighters.

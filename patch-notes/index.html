Pixel Battle

How it works →
v1.25 Nov 2026

Fighters learned to fight

  • NewFighters now think for themselves: they pick targets, circle, press forward and back off when hurt or outnumbered.
  • NewAttacks have a visible wind-up, so opponents can roll out of the way or raise a guard, then counter.
  • NewSlash arcs, dodge rolls, knockback, and floating DODGE, BLOCK and MISS text.
  • BalanceHealth retuned so most fighters are knocked out in combat instead of being finished by the zone.
  • ImprovedSmoother movement between frames.
  • NewThe verify page now has a match report: length, knockouts, dodges, blocks, most KOs and every elimination in order.
  • ImprovedVerify links now stay open for 7 days. After that, they lead to a page explaining the details were removed, while proof files keep working.
v1.14 Nov 2026

Names and more winners

  • ImprovedEvery fighter shows their name, with smaller labels in crowded fights.
  • New“How many winners?” lets the last few standing all win, up to 50.
v1.04 Nov 2026

Into the arena

  • NewUp to 100 pixel fighters in a shrinking zone, with HP bars, a kill feed and a zoom on the final fighters.

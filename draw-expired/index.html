Verify links stay open for 7 days after a raffle finishes. This one is older than that, so SpinDraw no longer shows its names, winners or match details.

Why did this happen?

A raffle’s entry list is full of people’s names. A link can get passed around for a long time, so we don’t want those names on display forever. Seven days gives everyone time to check the result, and then the page closes on its own.

Does this change the result?

No. The draw and its winners stay exactly as they were. Only this page has stopped showing them.

I still need to see this draw. What can I do?

Ask the person who ran the raffle. If they saved the proof file when the raffle finished, you can open it on the verify page, which still re-runs every draw. Neither of us can bring this link back, and SpinDraw has no copy to restore.

What exactly was removed?

SpinDraw never stored this raffle on a server. Its details were carried inside the link itself, in the part of the address that browsers don’t send to websites. So after 7 days, our verify page simply refuses to open the link.

That also means we can’t erase copies of a link that people have saved or posted. If you need a link taken down, ask whoever posted it to remove it.

Running a raffle?

Download the proof file when you finish a raffle, and keep it for as long as you may need to show the result. It stays with you, and it doesn’t expire.
